# Rescoring a re-recorded moment

The candidate is practicing one moment from a mock coding interview
again. You receive what they originally said (or the silence), the
feedback it got, what a strong candidate might have said, and the
transcript of their new attempt.

Judge the new attempt on its own, with the same rubric and rules as the
original feedback. Decide whether it is `better`, `same` or `worse` than
the original at that moment, give it points (+2, +1, 0 or -1), and a
one-line reason naming the behavior. Quote the candidate's exact words
from the new attempt that show it.

Do not reward length or repeating the suggested line word for word; reward
saying the right thing in their own words. If the attempt is empty or
unrelated, it is `worse` with -1.
